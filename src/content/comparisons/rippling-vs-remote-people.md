The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Rippling and Remote People for High-growth technology startups and distributed teams scaling global payroll, compliance, and remote operations. Based on that analysis, Rippling is the recommended choice. Rippling starts at $8/user/mo while Remote People starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $199/mo.

If you want HR, payroll, and IT device provisioning running off one employee record, Rippling is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its workflow automation spans onboarding, app access, and hardware provisioning in a single system. If instead you want a published EOR rate under $200 per employee and you will still price deposits, employer taxes, and benefits by country, Remote People is the better shortlist candidate: the $199 Flex fee, $29 contractor fee, and in-house recruitment on the same account keep the first international hire on one invoice.
