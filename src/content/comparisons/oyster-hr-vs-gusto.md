The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Oyster HR and Gusto for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Gusto is the recommended choice. Oyster HR starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $699/mo while Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user.

If your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the stronger call for Tech Startups: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams. If instead you are a mid-market remote team where admin and employee experience drive adoption, Oyster HR is the better shortlist candidate: its onboarding UX and employment cost calculator make first-time international hiring legible to non-specialists.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Gusto edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.6/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Oyster HR versus Gusto hub puts Oyster HR near $10,146 in year three against $26,672 for Gusto, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.
