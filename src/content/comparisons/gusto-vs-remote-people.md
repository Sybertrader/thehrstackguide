The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Gusto and Remote People for High-growth technology startups and distributed teams scaling global payroll, compliance, and remote operations. Based on that analysis, Remote People is the recommended choice. Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user while Remote People starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $199/mo.

If you want a published EOR rate under $200 per employee and you will still price deposits, employer taxes, and benefits by country, Remote People is the stronger call for Tech Startups: the $199 Flex fee, $29 contractor fee, and in-house recruitment on the same account keep the first international hire on one invoice. If instead your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the better shortlist candidate: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Remote People edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
