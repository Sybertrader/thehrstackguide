The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Breezy HR and Recruitee for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Recruitee is the recommended choice. Breezy HR starts at Free tier / $157/mo while Recruitee starts at $199/mo.

If you hire across European markets and need GDPR data residency alongside localized careers pages, Recruitee is the stronger call for Startups: EU data residency and multi-language employer branding are native here rather than retrofitted. If instead you are a small team that wants a visual pipeline live this week with no procurement cycle, Breezy HR is the better shortlist candidate: a free tier, Trello-style pipelines, and built-in async video screening get real hiring running without setup overhead.
