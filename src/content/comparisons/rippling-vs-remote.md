The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Rippling and Remote for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Remote is the recommended choice for Global EOR. Rippling starts at $8/user/mo while Remote starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo.

If your board treats IP ownership and compliance purity as non-negotiable, Remote is the stronger call on Global EOR for Tech Startups: it owns 100% of its local entities and folds IP assignment plus zero-markup FX into the base fee. If instead you want HR, payroll, and IT device provisioning running off one employee record, Rippling is the better shortlist candidate: its workflow automation spans onboarding, app access, and hardware provisioning in a single system.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Remote is the stronger fit for Global EOR for Tech Startups evaluating international remote team operations: Owns 100% of its local legal entities directly rather than relying on secondary EOR partners; Zero FX markup on cross-border payouts keeps international payroll costs predictable; and Built-in IP and invention-assignment protections purpose-built for distributed, IP-sensitive teams. Both platforms carry solid overall ratings (4.6/5 for Remote vs. 4.8/5 for Rippling), but Rippling is built primarily as a general-purpose domestic HRIS, so Remote pulls ahead specifically on Global EOR for globally distributed teams.
