The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Remote for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Remote starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead your board treats IP ownership and compliance purity as non-negotiable, Remote is the better shortlist candidate: it owns 100% of its local entities and folds IP assignment plus zero-markup FX into the base fee.
