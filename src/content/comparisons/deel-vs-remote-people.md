The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Remote People for High-growth technology startups and distributed teams scaling global payroll, compliance, and remote operations. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Remote People starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $199/mo.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead you want a published EOR rate under $200 per employee and you will still price deposits, employer taxes, and benefits by country, Remote People is the better shortlist candidate: the $199 Flex fee, $29 contractor fee, and in-house recruitment on the same account keep the first international hire on one invoice.
