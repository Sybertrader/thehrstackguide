The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Breezy HR and JazzHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Breezy HR is the recommended choice. Breezy HR starts at Free tier / $157/mo while JazzHR starts at $75/mo.

If you are a small team that wants a visual pipeline live this week with no procurement cycle, Breezy HR is the stronger call for Startups: a free tier, Trello-style pipelines, and built-in async video screening get real hiring running without setup overhead. If instead you are cost-constrained and want every interviewer in the system without paying per seat, JazzHR is the better shortlist candidate: unlimited free seats at $75/month removes the usual trade-off between budget and panel participation.
