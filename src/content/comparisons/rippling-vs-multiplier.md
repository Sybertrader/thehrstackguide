The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Rippling and Multiplier for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Rippling is the recommended choice. Rippling starts at $8/user/mo while Multiplier starts at $40/mo; EOR starts at $400/mo.

If you want HR, payroll, and IT device provisioning running off one employee record, Rippling is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its workflow automation spans onboarding, app access, and hardware provisioning in a single system. If instead you are hiring concentrated headcount across APAC or EMEA and price per seat is the deciding factor, Multiplier is the better shortlist candidate: it delivers instant localized contracts at one of the lowest published EOR rates in the category.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Rippling edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.8/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Rippling versus Multiplier hub puts Rippling near $4,645 in year three against $5,443 for Multiplier, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.
