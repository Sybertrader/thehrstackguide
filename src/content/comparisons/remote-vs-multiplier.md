The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Remote and Multiplier for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Multiplier is the recommended choice. Remote starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Multiplier starts at $40/mo; EOR starts at $400/mo.

If you are hiring concentrated headcount across APAC or EMEA and price per seat is the deciding factor, Multiplier is the stronger call for Tech Startups: it delivers instant localized contracts at one of the lowest published EOR rates in the category. If instead your board treats IP ownership and compliance purity as non-negotiable, Remote is the better shortlist candidate: it owns 100% of its local entities and folds IP assignment plus zero-markup FX into the base fee.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Multiplier edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
