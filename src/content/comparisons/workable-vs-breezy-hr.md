The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Workable and Breezy HR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Workable is the recommended choice. Workable starts at $299/mo while Breezy HR starts at Free tier / $157/mo.

If you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the stronger call for Startups: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product. If instead you are a small team that wants a visual pipeline live this week with no procurement cycle, Breezy HR is the better shortlist candidate: a free tier, Trello-style pipelines, and built-in async video screening get real hiring running without setup overhead.
