The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Gusto for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the better shortlist candidate: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Deel edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.8/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Deel versus Gusto hub puts Deel near $8,695 in year three against $26,672 for Gusto, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.

Deel starts at $49/mo (EOR starts at $599/mo) and is tagged Best for Global Scale & Owned Entities. Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user (US payroll Simple plan; no international EOR) and is tagged Best for US SMBs (1–50). Ratings in this file are 4.8 versus 4.6.
