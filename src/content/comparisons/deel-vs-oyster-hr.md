The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Oyster HR for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Oyster HR starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $699/mo.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead you are a mid-market remote team where admin and employee experience drive adoption, Oyster HR is the better shortlist candidate: its onboarding UX and employment cost calculator make first-time international hiring legible to non-specialists.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Deel edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.8/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
