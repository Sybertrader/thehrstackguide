The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Ashby and Workable for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Ashby is the recommended choice. Ashby starts at $400/mo while Workable starts at $299/mo.

If you want funnel analytics and a sourcing CRM inside the ATS rather than bolted on afterwards, Ashby is the stronger call for Startups: its reporting depth and unlimited-seat flat tiers reward teams that actually instrument their hiring funnel. If instead you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the better shortlist candidate: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Ashby edges out the competition for Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM, and strong support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.
