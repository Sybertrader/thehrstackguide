The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Oyster HR and Multiplier for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Multiplier is the recommended choice. Oyster HR starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $699/mo while Multiplier starts at $40/mo; EOR starts at $400/mo.

If you are hiring concentrated headcount across APAC or EMEA and price per seat is the deciding factor, Multiplier is the stronger call for Tech Startups: it delivers instant localized contracts at one of the lowest published EOR rates in the category. If instead you are a mid-market remote team where admin and employee experience drive adoption, Oyster HR is the better shortlist candidate: its onboarding UX and employment cost calculator make first-time international hiring legible to non-specialists.
