The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Workable and JazzHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Workable is the recommended choice. Workable starts at $299/mo while JazzHR starts at $75/mo.

If you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the stronger call for Startups: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product. If instead you are cost-constrained and want every interviewer in the system without paying per seat, JazzHR is the better shortlist candidate: unlimited free seats at $75/month removes the usual trade-off between budget and panel participation.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Workable edges ahead for Startups despite matching 4.4/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM—and stronger support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Workable versus JazzHR hub puts Workable near $4,340 in year three against $1,021 for JazzHR, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.

Workable starts at $299/mo (Standard plan from $299/mo (1–20 employees)) and is tagged Best for Built-in AI Candidate Sourcing. JazzHR starts at $75/mo (Tiered monthly plans from $75/mo) and is tagged Best for Budget SMB Unlimited Posting. Ratings in this file are 4.4 versus 4.4.

Workable covers AI Candidate Sourcing in this pairing while JazzHR does not. JazzHR covers Unlimited User Seats in this pairing while Workable does not.
