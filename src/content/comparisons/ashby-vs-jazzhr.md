The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Ashby and JazzHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Ashby is the recommended choice. Ashby starts at $400/mo while JazzHR starts at $75/mo.

If you want funnel analytics and a sourcing CRM inside the ATS rather than bolted on afterwards, Ashby is the stronger call for Startups: its reporting depth and unlimited-seat flat tiers reward teams that actually instrument their hiring funnel. If instead you are cost-constrained and want every interviewer in the system without paying per seat, JazzHR is the better shortlist candidate: unlimited free seats at $75/month removes the usual trade-off between budget and panel participation.
