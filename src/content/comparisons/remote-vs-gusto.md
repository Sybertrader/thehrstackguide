The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Remote and Gusto for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Remote is the recommended choice. Remote starts at $29/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user.

If your board treats IP ownership and compliance purity as non-negotiable, Remote is the stronger call for Tech Startups: it owns 100% of its local entities and folds IP assignment plus zero-markup FX into the base fee. If instead your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the better shortlist candidate: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Remote edges ahead for Tech Startups despite matching 4.6/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins—and stronger support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
