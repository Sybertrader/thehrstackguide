The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Workable and BambooHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Workable is the recommended choice. Workable starts at $299/mo while BambooHR is quote-based with no public starter price.

If you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the stronger call for Startups: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product. If instead you already run BambooHR as your HRIS and want hiring to live in the same system, BambooHR is the better shortlist candidate: candidate-to-employee conversion needs no re-entry because the candidate and the employee are one record.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Workable edges ahead for Startups despite matching 4.4/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM—and stronger support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.
