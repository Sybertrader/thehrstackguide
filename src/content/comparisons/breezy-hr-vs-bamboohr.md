The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Breezy HR and BambooHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Breezy HR is the recommended choice. Breezy HR starts at Free tier / $157/mo while BambooHR is quote-based with no public starter price.

If you are a small team that wants a visual pipeline live this week with no procurement cycle, Breezy HR is the stronger call for Startups: a free tier, Trello-style pipelines, and built-in async video screening get real hiring running without setup overhead. If instead you already run BambooHR as your HRIS and want hiring to live in the same system, BambooHR is the better shortlist candidate: candidate-to-employee conversion needs no re-entry because the candidate and the employee are one record.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Breezy HR edges ahead for Startups despite matching 4.4/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM—and stronger support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.
