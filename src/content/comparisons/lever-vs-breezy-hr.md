The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Lever and Breezy HR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Breezy HR is the recommended choice. Lever is quote-based with no public starter price while Breezy HR starts at Free tier / $157/mo.

If you are a small team that wants a visual pipeline live this week with no procurement cycle, Breezy HR is the stronger call for Startups: a free tier, Trello-style pipelines, and built-in async video screening get real hiring running without setup overhead. If instead your hiring depends on outbound sourcing and re-engaging candidates you already know, Lever is the better shortlist candidate: its ATS and candidate CRM are genuinely unified, so nurture campaigns run off the same pipeline data.
