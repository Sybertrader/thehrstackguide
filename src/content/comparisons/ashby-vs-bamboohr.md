The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Ashby and BambooHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Ashby is the recommended choice. Ashby starts at $400/mo while BambooHR is quote-based with no public starter price.

If you want funnel analytics and a sourcing CRM inside the ATS rather than bolted on afterwards, Ashby is the stronger call for Startups: its reporting depth and unlimited-seat flat tiers reward teams that actually instrument their hiring funnel. If instead you already run BambooHR as your HRIS and want hiring to live in the same system, BambooHR is the better shortlist candidate: candidate-to-employee conversion needs no re-entry because the candidate and the employee are one record.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Ashby edges out the competition for Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM, and strong support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.
