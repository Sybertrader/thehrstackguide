The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Workable and Recruitee for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Recruitee is the recommended choice. Workable starts at $299/mo while Recruitee starts at $199/mo.

If you hire across European markets and need GDPR data residency alongside localized careers pages, Recruitee is the stronger call for Startups: EU data residency and multi-language employer branding are native here rather than retrofitted. If instead you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the better shortlist candidate: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Recruitee edges out the competition for Startups due to its superior score (4.5/5), strength in interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM, and strong support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Workable versus Recruitee hub puts Workable near $4,340 in year three against $2,708 for Recruitee, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.

Workable starts at $299/mo (Standard plan from $299/mo (1–20 employees)) and is tagged Best for Built-in AI Candidate Sourcing. Recruitee starts at $199/mo (Launch plan from $199/mo (job-slot based, unlimited users)) and is tagged Best for EU GDPR Data Residency & Employer Brand. Ratings in this file are 4.4 versus 4.5.

Workable covers AI Candidate Sourcing in this pairing while Recruitee does not. Recruitee covers Multi-Language Support in this pairing while Workable does not.
