The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Gusto and Papaya Global for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Gusto is the recommended choice. Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user while Papaya Global starts at $25/mo; EOR starts at $650/mo.

If your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the stronger call for Tech Startups: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams. If instead your finance team needs consolidated payroll reporting across owned entities and EOR workers in 20+ countries, Papaya Global is the better shortlist candidate: its workforce intelligence layer and ERP-grade controls are built for exactly that reporting problem.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Gusto edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.6/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
