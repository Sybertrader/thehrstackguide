The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Ashby and Lever for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Ashby is the recommended choice. Ashby starts at $400/mo while Lever is quote-based with no public starter price.

If you want funnel analytics and a sourcing CRM inside the ATS rather than bolted on afterwards, Ashby is the stronger call for Startups: its reporting depth and unlimited-seat flat tiers reward teams that actually instrument their hiring funnel. If instead your hiring depends on outbound sourcing and re-engaging candidates you already know, Lever is the better shortlist candidate: its ATS and candidate CRM are genuinely unified, so nurture campaigns run off the same pipeline data.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Ashby edges out the competition for Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM, and strong support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.
