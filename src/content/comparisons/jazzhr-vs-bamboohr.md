The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for JazzHR and BambooHR for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, JazzHR is the recommended choice. JazzHR starts at $75/mo while BambooHR is quote-based with no public starter price.

If you are cost-constrained and want every interviewer in the system without paying per seat, JazzHR is the stronger call for Startups: unlimited free seats at $75/month removes the usual trade-off between budget and panel participation. If instead you already run BambooHR as your HRIS and want hiring to live in the same system, BambooHR is the better shortlist candidate: candidate-to-employee conversion needs no re-entry because the candidate and the employee are one record.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, JazzHR edges ahead for Startups despite matching 4.4/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM—and stronger support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this JazzHR versus BambooHR hub puts JazzHR near $1,089 in year three against $8,900 for BambooHR, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.
