The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Ashby and Recruitee for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Ashby is the recommended choice. Ashby starts at $400/mo while Recruitee starts at $199/mo.

If you want funnel analytics and a sourcing CRM inside the ATS rather than bolted on afterwards, Ashby is the stronger call for Startups: its reporting depth and unlimited-seat flat tiers reward teams that actually instrument their hiring funnel. If instead you hire across European markets and need GDPR data residency alongside localized careers pages, Recruitee is the better shortlist candidate: EU data residency and multi-language employer branding are native here rather than retrofitted.
