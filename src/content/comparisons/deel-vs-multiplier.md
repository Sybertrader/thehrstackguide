The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Multiplier for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Multiplier starts at $40/mo; EOR starts at $400/mo.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead you are hiring concentrated headcount across APAC or EMEA and price per seat is the deciding factor, Multiplier is the better shortlist candidate: it delivers instant localized contracts at one of the lowest published EOR rates in the category.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Deel edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.8/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
