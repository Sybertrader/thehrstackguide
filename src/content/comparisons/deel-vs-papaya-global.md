The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Deel and Papaya Global for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Deel is the recommended choice. Deel starts at $49/mo; EOR starts at $599/mo while Papaya Global starts at $25/mo; EOR starts at $650/mo.

If you are hiring full-time employees in several countries at once and want one vendor to own entities, contracts, and equipment, Deel is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its 150+ country owned-entity network and 24-hour onboarding remove the entity-setup bottleneck entirely. If instead your finance team needs consolidated payroll reporting across owned entities and EOR workers in 20+ countries, Papaya Global is the better shortlist candidate: its workforce intelligence layer and ERP-grade controls are built for exactly that reporting problem.
