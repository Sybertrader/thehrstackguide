The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Gusto and Multiplier for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Multiplier is the recommended choice. Gusto starts at $49/mo + $6/user while Multiplier starts at $40/mo; EOR starts at $400/mo.

If you are hiring concentrated headcount across APAC or EMEA and price per seat is the deciding factor, Multiplier is the stronger call for Tech Startups: it delivers instant localized contracts at one of the lowest published EOR rates in the category. If instead your workforce is US-based and you want payroll, tax filings, and benefits to run themselves, Gusto is the better shortlist candidate: automated federal, state, and local filings come standard with the cleanest admin experience for lean teams.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Multiplier edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.7/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Gusto versus Multiplier hub puts Gusto near $28,450 in year three against $5,443 for Multiplier, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.
