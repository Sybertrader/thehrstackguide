The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Rippling and Papaya Global for Seed to Series B fast-growing technology companies hiring remote engineers worldwide. Based on that analysis, Rippling is the recommended choice. Rippling starts at $8/user/mo while Papaya Global starts at $25/mo; EOR starts at $650/mo.

If you want HR, payroll, and IT device provisioning running off one employee record, Rippling is the stronger call for Tech Startups: its workflow automation spans onboarding, app access, and hardware provisioning in a single system. If instead your finance team needs consolidated payroll reporting across owned entities and EOR workers in 20+ countries, Papaya Global is the better shortlist candidate: its workforce intelligence layer and ERP-grade controls are built for exactly that reporting problem.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Rippling edges out the competition for Tech Startups due to its superior score (4.8/5), strength in entity ownership, global payroll routing, and FX conversion margins, and strong support for IP ownership, fast onboarding, and equity option distribution.
