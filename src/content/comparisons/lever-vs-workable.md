The HR Stack Guide research team evaluated 2026 feature sets and pricing models for Lever and Workable for Early-stage startups building their first structured hiring process without a dedicated recruiting team. Based on that analysis, Workable is the recommended choice. Lever is quote-based with no public starter price while Workable starts at $299/mo.

If you want to post widely and source proactively without sitting through a sales call, Workable is the stronger call for Startups: one-click syndication to 200+ job boards plus a 400M-profile sourcing database ships in the base product. If instead your hiring depends on outbound sourcing and re-engaging candidates you already know, Lever is the better shortlist candidate: its ATS and candidate CRM are genuinely unified, so nurture campaigns run off the same pipeline data.

Based on The HR Stack Guide's 2026 evaluation, Workable edges ahead for Startups despite matching 4.4/5 overall scores, because it covers more of the capabilities that matter here—interview scorecards, candidate pipeline stages, and sourcing CRM—and stronger support for fast setup, transparent pricing, and flexible seat-based collaboration.

Modeled three-year platform escalation for this Lever versus Workable hub puts Lever near $13,252 in year three against $4,069 for Workable, using published seat math rather than a sales-deck average.
